# The Bowl - Rules Document

## Prerequisites:

- Minimum players: 2
- Maximum players: ~14 (Not fixed, technically unlimited)
- Separate players into two teams with roughly the same number of participants in each team

## Pre-Game:

- Create a set of 3-5 words per player (More words = Longer game)
- Words must be THINGS or VERBS only

## Game:

The game is divided into three sections. Each section consists of multiple rounds where teams alternate taking 1-minute turns. The objective is to guess as many words as possible from the BOWL. After each 1-minute round, the BOWL passes to the other team for their round. This continues until no words remain in the BOWL. Once no words remain in the BOWL, move to the next section.

**Note:** A round is the 1-minute turn given to the active team. A section determines what rules apply to how players can give clues during that round.

**App note:** The app tracks the active team, timer, bowl, and score. Players decide among themselves who is holding the phone and giving clues for a round.

## Sections:

**Section 1: Explain with Words**

- Describe the word using other words
- No acting, no sounds, no pointing
- Cannot use the word itself or the word in other languages

**Section 2: One Word Only**

- You can only say ONE word as a clue
- Cannot use the word itself or the word in other languages

**Section 3: Charades**

- Use only body language and gestures
- No sounds or speaking allowed
- Can point to objects in the room if needed

## Scoring:

- 1 point per word guessed correctly
- Keep score for each section and for the full match
- In the app, section totals are shown at section transitions and in the final game summary

## Winning:

- Team with the most total points after all 3 sections wins the match
- Keep section scores for transparency, but total points decide the winner
- If total points are tied, play sudden-death rounds until one team outscores the other in a full alternation cycle

## Important:

- If at any moment during a round, a person makes filler noises such as "uh", "ummmm", or a long "hmmmm...", the round ends immediately and the BOWL passes to the other team
- In the app, fouls are handled socially by the players and use the same `End round` action. There is no dedicated foul button or stored foul reason.
- If a word is skipped, it re-enters the bowl immediately, shuffled among the remaining words, with at least one different word before it
- The bowl order is reshuffled between rounds
- One word means one word:
  - Apostrophes count as one word (`don't` is valid)
  - Hyphenated terms count as two words (`ice-cream` is invalid)
  - Multi-word phrases are invalid (`Star Wars`, `credit card`)
